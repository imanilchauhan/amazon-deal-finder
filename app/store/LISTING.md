**Full description:**

CartCrush finds the best real discounts on Amazon India every day and shows them in one clean, fast app.

WHY CARTCRUSH
• Real price drops only: every deal shows the price, the original price and how much you save.
• One card per product: no repeats, no colour-and-size spam.
• Prices with a timestamp: you always see when a price was last checked, and old prices are hidden.
• Deal of the hour, winter picks, steals under ₹499, gifts for her, home and kitchen and more.

FIND THE RIGHT DEAL FAST
• Ask in plain words: "gift for mom under 500", "jacket under 1000 for dad".
• Browse by department, sort by biggest discount, lowest price or newest.
• Curated collections that stay useful all season.

SAVE AND SHARE
• Tap the heart to save deals. Saved deals stay on your phone, no account needed.
• Share any deal with friends and family in one tap.

ONE NOTIFICATION A DAY, AT MOST
Turn on the daily "today's new deals" notification if you like. No price-tracking spam.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No sign-in, no ads, no tracking. Your saved deals never leave your phone.

IMPORTANT
CartCrush is an independent deal finder and is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with Amazon. As an Amazon Associate, CartCrush earns from qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you. Prices and availability change often; the price on Amazon at the time of purchase applies.
